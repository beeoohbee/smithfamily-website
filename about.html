Our story

The Smith family has called the Cameron area home for years. Between us, we've picked up a wide range of skills — from technology and websites to marketing, beekeeping, and just plain getting things organized.

Over time, friends, neighbors, and local business owners started asking us for help. Smith Family Co. is our way of making that official: one place to reach a family of people who know their stuff and genuinely want to see our community do well.

What we believe

  • Small businesses are the heart of small towns. When they succeed, everyone in Cameron benefits.
  • Good help shouldn't require a big-city budget. We keep our pricing fair and our solutions practical.
  • Treat people like neighbors — because they are.
