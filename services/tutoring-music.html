Learning that builds confidence

Whether a student is learning their first scale, stuck on algebra homework, or getting ready for the ACT, one-on-one help makes a real difference. We meet students where they are, explain things in a way that clicks, and help them build the habits and confidence to keep improving on their own.

How it works

  1. Tell us what the student needs. An instrument, a class, or a test date.
  2. First session. We get to know the student and where they're at.
  3. A plan. Regular sessions focused on what will help most, with practice between sessions.
  4. Progress updates for parents along the way.
  • Saxophone with Landyn or Ian, from first notes to band, jazz band, and auditions.
  • Trumpet with Ethan, for beginners through advanced players.
  • Math & science with Landyn, from homework help to real understanding.
  • ACT prep with Landyn: practice tests, pacing, and test-day strategies.
