Keep it running

Regular maintenance is the cheapest repair there is. A tune-up before mowing season, fresh oil and filters, and a look over your equipment can prevent a breakdown at the worst possible time. When something does break, we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth fixing.

  • Farm chores. Livestock care, fencing, hauling hay, field work, and seasonal jobs.
  • Small engines. Mowers, tillers, chainsaws, trimmers, and generators.
  • Vehicles & equipment. Cars, trucks, ATVs, and farm equipment.
  • Honest advice. Repair or replace — we'll give you a straight answer.
