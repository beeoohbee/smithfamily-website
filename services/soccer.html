Better touches, smarter play, more fun

Team practices only have so much time for each player. Extra training with a coach who knows the game helps players sharpen the fundamentals, try new skills without pressure, and show up to their next game more confident.

What a session looks like

Sessions are built around the player. We start by watching them play, talk about their goals, and then work on the skills that will make the biggest difference — mixing in drills, small games, and plenty of touches on the ball.

Mentoring goes beyond the drills: talking through game situations, handling nerves and setbacks, being a good teammate, and what it takes to move up to club and high school teams.

  • All ages and levels. From first-timers to high school players.
  • One-on-one or small groups. Train alone or bring a friend or teammate.
  • Skills that carry over. Ball control, passing, shooting, footwork, and fitness.
  • Positive and encouraging. Confidence and sportsmanship come first.
