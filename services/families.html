A little help at home goes a long way

New devices, a pile of paperwork, a calendar that's out of control, or worries about what the kids (or grandparents) are clicking on — everyday life at home comes with plenty of small projects that are easier with a hand. We come to you, take our time, and make sure you're comfortable before we leave.

Patient, judgment-free help

There's no such thing as a silly question. Whether you're setting up your first smartphone or organizing years of digital photos, we go at your pace and write down the steps so you can do it again on your own.

  • Home tech with Bob: devices, smart TVs, printers, and home Wi-Fi.
  • Online safety with Bob: parental controls, password managers, and spotting scams.
  • Family organization with Kate: budgets, photos, important documents, and the family calendar.
