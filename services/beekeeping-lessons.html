Start your beekeeping journey with a guide

Beekeeping is rewarding, but the first year can be overwhelming — what equipment to buy, when to inspect, what's normal and what's a problem. Learning from local beekeepers who work with bees in the same climate you do takes a lot of the guesswork out.

What you'll learn

Beginners start with the basics and work up to confidently inspecting their own hives. Mentoring picks up wherever you are, right at your own hives, so you learn on the bees you're actually keeping.

Our talks and hive visits are a fun, hands-on introduction for schools, 4-H, scouts, and community groups.

  • Bee biology and how a colony works.
  • Choosing equipment and setting up your first hive.
  • Hive inspections and spotting pests and disease.
  • Through the seasons — from spring build-up to getting through winter.

Have bees where you don't want them? See swarm and hive removal.
