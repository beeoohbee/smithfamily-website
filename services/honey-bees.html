Bees in the wrong place? Don't spray — call us.

Honey bees are important pollinators for gardens and farms. If a swarm has landed in a tree or bush, or a colony has moved into a wall, shed, or outbuilding, we'll safely remove the bees alive and relocate them to our own apiary, where they can keep doing what they do best.

Found a swarm?

Swarms usually rest in one spot for a short time while they look for a new home, so the sooner you reach out, the better. In the meantime, keep people and pets at a distance and don't spray them.

Not sure if they're honey bees? Send us a photo and where they are, and we'll let you know.

  • Swarms clustered on trees, bushes, fences, and equipment.
  • Established colonies in walls, sheds, and outbuildings.
  • Relocated, not killed. Bees go to our own apiary.
  • Raw, unfiltered honey from our hives — ask what's available this season.
